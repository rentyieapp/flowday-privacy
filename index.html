FlowDay: Calm Daily Planner

Official Privacy Policy & Terms of Service • Last updated: November 22, 2026
Summary: FlowDay is built with a privacy-first mindset. Your daily tasks, focus timer stats, breathing exercises, and personal plans are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

FlowDay is designed so you can use all features without creating a mandatory account. Depending on how you use our service:

  • Local User Data: Daily routine checklists, Pomodoro timer sessions, breathing cycles, squad codes, and local tasks remain stored securely on your own device.
  • Advertising & Analytics: For non-paying (free tier) users, FlowDay displays ads powered by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID) / Apple IDFA to serve contextual ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.
  • Pro Subscriptions: Subscriptions are managed directly by Google Play Billing on Android and Apple App Store on iOS. We do not store or process your financial payment cards. Pro users enjoy a 100% ad-free experience.

2. Data Security & Encryption

All network communications (such as verifying subscription receipts and loading ad creatives) are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). We employ robust measures to safeguard information against unauthorized access.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app incorporates the following trusted third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads to free users. Google's privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing & Apple StoreKit: For secure digital subscription processing.

4. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion

Since your routine data is kept locally on your device, deleting the FlowDay application immediately purges all stored routines and timers from your device. If you wish to request full data deletion or have privacy inquiries, you can reach out directly to our support team.

5. Children's Privacy

FlowDay is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Terms of Service

By using FlowDay, you agree that the app is provided "as is" to help structure your daily productivity. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours prior to the end of the current billing cycle through your Google Play or App Store account settings.
